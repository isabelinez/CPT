FAQ

Where do sessions take place?

Personal training is mobile. I come to you at your home gym, a suitable park or field, or another agreed-upon location.

Do session packages expire?

Yes. All packages expire 6 months from the purchase date. Unused sessions cannot be carried over after expiration.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please provide 24 hours’ notice to cancel or reschedule. Late cancellations and missed sessions may count as a used session.

How does online coaching work?

After signing up, complete an intake and receive your personalized training program, exercise demonstrations, weekly check-ins, progress tracking, and program adjustments.

How does monthly billing and cancellation work?

Online Coaching is billed monthly through Square with a 4-month commitment. The subscription automatically ends after four billing cycles. A one-month pause is available if needed.

How does the nutrition consultation work?

Nutrition Kickstart is a 45-minute online consultation focused on your goals, eating habits, and practical nutrition strategies.

Can I receive nutrition coaching with a medical condition?

Nutrition services provide general nutrition guidance and are not medical nutrition therapy. For medical or therapeutic nutrition needs, please work with your physician or a registered dietitian.

How does billing work for Nutrition Coaching?

Nutrition Coaching is billed monthly through Square with a 4-month commitment. The subscription automatically ends after four billing cycles. A one-month pause is available if needed.

What happens if my payment fails?

Square may retry a failed payment. Please update your payment method promptly. Services may be paused until payment is successfully processed.

Are digital products refundable?

No. All digital products are final sale and non-refundable.

How do I receive digital products?

Digital products are sent to the email address provided at checkout. Check your spam or junk folder if you don’t receive it.

How do I contact you?

Use the Contact section below and include your name and what you need help with. Responses are typically sent within 1–2 business days.
