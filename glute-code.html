Why a program

Questions.

What equipment do I need?

A typical commercial gym: barbell, plates and a squat rack, a flat bench, dumbbells, leg press, leg extension, leg curl, hip abduction and adduction machines, a cable station and a 45° back extension bench. Every exercise includes substitutions if something isn’t available.

What experience level is it for?

Beginner to intermediate. You’ll start with weights that leave a few reps in reserve and progress from there. Each exercise has step-by-step instructions, and the squat can be done as a goblet squat or a barbell back squat.

How often do I train, and for how long?

Three days per week, ideally Monday, Wednesday and Friday, for eight weeks. Most sessions take about 55–65 minutes including the warm-up.

How do I receive the program?

It’s a digital PDF delivered to the email address you use at checkout. Check your spam or junk folder if you don’t see it. If you still can’t find it, email IsabelRodriguezdblr@gmail.com.

Can I get a refund?

Because this is a digital product, all sales are final once it has been delivered. If there’s a problem with your file or download, email me and I’ll help.

Is individual coaching included?

No. THE GLUTE CODE is a self-guided program. It doesn’t include individualized coaching, personal training, injury rehabilitation or medical advice. If you want a program built for you with weekly check-ins, see online coaching.

Does it include nutrition?

Yes, a practical nutrition guide covering protein, meal building, sample meals and how to track trends. It’s general education, not an individualized meal plan or medical nutrition therapy.
